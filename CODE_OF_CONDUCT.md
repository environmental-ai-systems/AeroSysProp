# Code of conduct

AeroSysProp is developed by a community of researchers, students and
software developers from many institutions and countries. We want taking
part to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their background,
career stage or level of experience.

## Our standards

We expect everyone taking part in the project, in issues, pull requests,
discussions, meetings and any other project space, to:

- be respectful and considerate, including in disagreement;
- give and take criticism of work constructively, and keep it about the
  work, not the person;
- credit the work and ideas of others;
- be patient with newcomers and with questions;
- respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or behaviour, and
publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

This code adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which sets out these standards and how they are enforced in more detail.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to a
member of the core team listed in [METHODS_GOVERNANCE.md](METHODS_GOVERNANCE.md),
or by email to [PROJECT CONTACT ADDRESS]. Reports will be handled in
confidence. A core team member who is the subject of a report will take no
part in handling it.

## Enforcement

The core team will review every report and respond in proportion to what
happened, from a private conversation to a temporary or permanent ban from
project spaces, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor
Covenant.
